The tutorial covers the path from post-quantum cryptographic primitives to a
working RISC-V platform on FPGA: where the cryptographic work belongs in the
system, how accelerators attach to a RISC-V core, and what the resulting
platform costs in area, performance and energy on constrained IoT hardware.
