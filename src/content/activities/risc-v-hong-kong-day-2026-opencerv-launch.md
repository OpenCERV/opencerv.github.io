## A new community begins

The **CityUHK-EE RISC-V Open Community (OpenCERV)** launched at **RISC-V Hong Kong
Day 2026** on **21 October 2026**. The launch marks the beginning of an open
community connecting research, education, open-source development and the RISC-V
ecosystem in Hong Kong.

## Building together

OpenCERV focuses on two technical pillars: **Edge AI** and **Security**. The
community aims to bring people together around open reference platforms,
hardware IP, benchmarks, tutorials and demonstrations, keeping technical
collaboration moving between events.

## Global leaders at RISC-V Hong Kong Day

Global leaders, speakers and community representatives attended RISC-V Hong Kong
Day 2026 and showed their support for the launch of OpenCERV. Participants came
from a wide range of influential organisations, including
**City University of Hong Kong**, **RISC-V International**, the **Hong Kong Institute of Science &
Innovation, Chinese Academy of Sciences**, **BayLibre**, **Red Hat**,
**DeepComputing**, **Tenstorrent**, **Linux Foundation APAC**, **CNCF China**,
**SpaceMIT**, and the **OpenHW Foundation / Eclipse Foundation**.

Thank you to everyone who joined us for the event.
